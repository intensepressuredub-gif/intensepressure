The project

Intense Pressure is Brazilian saxophonist and multi-instrumentalist Vito Lorenzoni's reggae/dub project — connecting Florianópolis roots to a base in Cremona, Italy.

Every beat, timbre and layer is captured and folded in front of the room, so the arrangement grows the way a DJ set breathes — but built entirely by hand, from a saxophone and acoustic instruments up.

Instrumental precision meets the flow of a live DJ set.

The method

How the show is built

1
